How Is Gratuity Calculated in the UAE?

Under UAE Labour Law, gratuity is based on your basic salary (not your total package) and your length of service:

  • 21 days' basic salary for each year of the first 5 years of service
  • 30 days' basic salary for each additional year beyond 5 years
  • Total gratuity is capped at 2 years' total salary
  • Employees who resign before completing 1 year of service are not entitled to gratuity

This calculator gives an estimate for guidance only and is not legal advice. Always confirm your exact entitlement with UAE MOHRE or a legal advisor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is gratuity calculated on basic salary or total salary?

Gratuity is calculated only on your basic salary — housing allowance, transport allowance, and other benefits are excluded. Check your employment contract to confirm your basic salary component.

What happens if I resign before completing 1 year?

Under UAE Labour Law, employees who resign before completing one full year of continuous service are not entitled to any gratuity payment.

Does gratuity differ between limited and unlimited contracts?

Since the 2022 UAE Labour Law reform, all contracts are now limited-term, and the same gratuity formula applies regardless of how your contract is structured.

Is there a maximum gratuity amount?

Yes. Total gratuity payable cannot exceed two years' worth of your total salary, regardless of how many years you worked.

Does unpaid leave affect my gratuity calculation?

Yes, periods of unpaid leave are generally not counted toward your years of service for gratuity purposes. This calculator assumes continuous paid service.

Is this calculator accurate for MOHRE and Free Zone employees?

This tool follows the standard federal UAE Labour Law formula used by MOHRE-registered companies. Some free zones (like DIFC or ADGM) have slightly different rules, so double-check with your specific free zone authority if you work there.
